## Research

My research integrates UAV/drone remote sensing, spatio-temporal statistics, and machine learning for environmental and societal applications — including coastal and seagrass ecosystem monitoring, wildfire mapping, urban heat, and crime prediction. I direct the **GeoFly Lab**, and I teach GIS, remote sensing, and geospatial analysis (ENVS/GIST 115/215 A–C) at UC Santa Cruz.
